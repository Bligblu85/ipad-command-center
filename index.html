Supplements & recovery

Check off each reminder when completed. These notes summarize Leroy Colbert's suggestions, not prescribed dosages.

L-Arginine

Colbert suggests at least 10 g daily; this is not a universal safe or proven dose.

L-Glutamine

Colbert suggests at least 2 g daily and separating amino acids from protein. These timing claims are not established requirements. Check medication interactions before using supplements, especially with blood-pressure or erectile-dysfunction medicines.
