Supplements & recovery

Daily checklist

Reference reminders from Leroy Colbert's presentations. These are not prescribed doses; confirm supplement suitability and dosing with your clinician.

L-Arginine

Colbert suggests taking it between meals and before bed, away from protein. His suggested total is at least 10 g/day; that is not a universal safe or necessary dose. May interact with blood-pressure and erectile-dysfunction medications.

L-Glutamine

Colbert suggests 2 g/day, away from protein, and before/after training. Benefits for muscle growth and recovery are not well established in adequately nourished adults.

Recovery reminder: avoid overtraining and allow adequate rest. Workout-specific reminders apply only on training days.
